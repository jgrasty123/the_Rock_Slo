About
The Rock

Our Story

Built for
the Central Coast

The Rock has been a cornerstone of San Luis Obispo's live music and events scene for years — and under new ownership, it's entering its next chapter. Part concert venue, part neighborhood restaurant, part event space: built for people who believe every week deserves a great show, a great meal, and a great reason to gather.

Located at 855 Aerovista Place, The Rock features one of the largest outdoor event spaces in SLO County — extensive landscaping, fire pits, a professional stage, and an indoor/outdoor flow that makes every visit memorable.
