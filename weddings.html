Say Yes
at The Rock

SLO's Most Unforgettable Wedding Venue

Where SLO Couples
Start Their Story

The Rock is unlike any wedding venue on the Central Coast. Natural rock formations as your ceremony backdrop. A dramatic indoor stage for your first dance. String lights over the beer garden. Custom-monogrammed whiskey barrels at the bar. It's a venue that feels like yours from the moment you arrive — and looks stunning in every photo.

Located at 855 Aerovista Place in San Luis Obispo, we host a limited number of weddings each year to ensure every couple gets our full attention and the best possible experience.
