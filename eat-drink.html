SLO Brewing Co.

SLO Brew has been pouring for this community for over 35 years. Their flagship Superbloom Pale Ale and rotating seasonal taps are always flowing cold at The Rock — because what's a great venue without great beer?

Rod & Hammer Whiskey

California's craft whiskey, distilled in San Luis Obispo. Every old fashioned, sour, and neat pour you order at The Rock is R&H — aged in oak, built for this climate, and made to be sipped on a warm SLO evening.
