Endless Possibilities.

More than a venue, The Rock SLO is where unforgettable moments come to life.

Nestled in the heart of California's Central Coast, The Rock SLO offers a collection of distinctive indoor and outdoor event spaces designed for celebrations of every style and size. From elegant weddings and corporate gatherings to milestone celebrations and unforgettable social events, our experienced team brings every detail together with seamless planning, exceptional cuisine, and genuine hospitality.

With multiple venue options, award-worthy catering, full-service bar programs, professional event staff, in-house rentals, and state-of-the-art audio visual capabilities, everything you need is in one place — allowing you to focus on making memories while we take care of the rest.

Whether you're planning for 20 guests or 600, The Rock SLO is where extraordinary events begin.

Food Worth Talking About

Exceptional Cuisine,
Crafted for Your Celebration

At The Rock, we believe memorable events begin with exceptional food. Our culinary team prepares every menu fresh, on-site, using high-quality ingredients and locally sourced produce and meats whenever possible.

Whether you're envisioning elegant passed hors d'oeuvres, beautifully displayed stationary appetizers, interactive food stations, abundant buffet service, refined plated dinners, or warm family-style dining, we offer thoughtfully curated menu packages designed to complement every style of celebration.

Looking for something unique? We know every event is different. If you don't see exactly what you're looking for, our Executive Chef will work with you to create a custom menu tailored to your vision, tastes, dietary preferences, and budget.

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, our commitment to fresh ingredients, exceptional hospitality, and unforgettable flavors ensures your guests will be talking about the meal long after the event ends.

Who You'll Work With

Meet Todd Eads

With more than 30 years in hospitality, catering, and event management, Todd Eads has led event sales and operations for premier venues, luxury clubs, and award-winning catering companies. As Director of Event Sales & Operations at The Rock SLO, he oversees every aspect of the client journey — from the initial tour and planning process through event execution.

Known for his collaborative leadership style and attention to detail, Todd works closely with clients, vendors, and The Rock's culinary and operations teams to ensure every event reflects each client's unique vision.
