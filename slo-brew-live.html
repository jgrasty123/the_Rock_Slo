SLO Brew Live

The Venue

The Central Coast's
Finest Music Venue

For nearly three decades, SLO Brew has brought music lovers authentic, intimate performances from local bands and world-famous musicians alike. The stage has hosted artists including Neil Young, Snoop Dogg, and Sublime.

Today SLO Brew Live at The Rock features full concert lighting, exceptional acoustics, and one of the largest LED screens between Los Angeles and San Francisco — plus a full kitchen and bar just steps from the stage.

Meet Ryan

Ryan Orr
Talent Buyer · SLO Brew Live

Ryan Orr is a music industry executive with more than 20 years of experience in live entertainment, venue management, concert production, and strategic partnerships. He began his career working with the Dave Matthews Band organization, where he helped coordinate national promotions, large-scale events, artist logistics, and media initiatives. Throughout his career, Ryan has collaborated with a wide range of acclaimed artists and industry professionals while producing memorable live experiences across the country.

Known for his leadership, creative vision, and ability to execute complex productions, Ryan has built a reputation for developing successful events, cultivating lasting industry relationships, and creating meaningful connections between artists, audiences, and communities. Today, he continues to lead concert programming, event production, and business development on California's Central Coast.
